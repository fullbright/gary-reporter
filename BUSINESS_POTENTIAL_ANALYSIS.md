## 🚨 Critical Success Factors

1. **Pick 1-2 products maximum** - Don't spread too thin
2. **Validate before building** - Talk to customers first
3. **Focus on distribution** - Product is 20%, distribution is 80%
4. **Start with problem, not technology** - Focus on pain points
5. **Price based on value** - Don't underprice
6. **Build in public** - Use social media for marketing
7. **Automate everything** - Keep operations lean
