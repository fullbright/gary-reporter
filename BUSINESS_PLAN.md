# Gary Reporter - Business Plan

## Executive Summary

**Gary Reporter** is a Bible research and analysis platform that combines Natural Language Processing (NLP), article curation, and automated reporting to help religious scholars, pastors, theological students, and Bible study groups conduct in-depth biblical research efficiently.

### Vision
To become the leading AI-powered Bible research platform that democratizes access to advanced biblical scholarship tools.

### Mission
Empower individuals and organizations to discover deeper insights from biblical texts through intelligent automation, NLP analysis, and comprehensive research tools.

## 7. Risk Analysis

### Major Risks and Mitigation

#### 1. Market Risk: Slow Adoption
**Risk**: Conservative religious market slow to adopt new technology
**Mitigation**:
- Partner with trusted institutions early
- Leverage testimonials and social proof
- Offer generous free tier
- Focus on ease of use and onboarding

#### 2. Competition Risk: Logos Responds
**Risk**: Market leader launches competing AI features
**Mitigation**:
- Move fast, establish brand early
- Focus on modern UX (their weakness)
- Build community and switching costs
- Patent key innovations
- Target underserved segments (students, small churches)

#### 3. Technical Risk: NLP Quality
**Risk**: NLP analysis produces poor results, hurts credibility
**Mitigation**:
- Extensive testing with domain experts
- Human review of automated outputs
- Clear disclaimers about limitations
- Continuous improvement with user feedback
- Hire biblical scholars as advisors

#### 4. Financial Risk: Runway Exhaustion
**Risk**: Running out of money before product-market fit
**Mitigation**:
- Maintain 12-month runway minimum
- Milestone-based fundraising
- Focus on revenue early
- Keep burn rate low in early stages

#### 5. Regulatory Risk: Copyright Issues
**Risk**: Bible translation copyright claims
**Mitigation**:
- License official translations
- Use public domain translations initially
- Partner with publishers
- Clear terms of service
- Legal review

#### 6. Talent Risk: Difficulty Hiring
**Risk**: Hard to find developers who understand religious context
**Mitigation**:
- Hire for technical skills, train on domain
- Build network in Christian tech community
- Offer competitive compensation
- Remote-first company (larger talent pool)
- Intern programs with seminaries
